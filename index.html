Special Rule: Alif (ا / أ) — Non-Connecting Letter

Alif is a one-way connector. It connects to the letter before it, but NEVER connects to any letter coming after it!

Asad (Lion)

كَأْس

Alif connects left only
بَاب
Baab (Door)
Alif breaks connection
